FAQ

Will I see my data on a new phone?
Not yet. Data is encrypted with this device's keys and excluded from backups. Export your expenses to CSV before changing phones.

The receipt QR code is not recognised.
The app reads QR codes of Ukrainian fiscal receipts that link to the tax office cabinet. Other QR codes are not receipts. If the code is damaged, photograph the receipt or enter the amount manually.

Recognition got an amount or an item wrong.
It happens with crumpled or faded receipts. On the receipt review screen fix the name, price or quantity and untick items that are not part of the renovation.

How do I delete all data?
Settings → Delete everything. The encryption keys are destroyed first, so the data cannot be recovered.
