One renovation, one number: what is left and where it went.

Renovo tracks an apartment renovation budget: expenses by room and category, receipts by QR or photo, an acceptance checklist. All data stays on your phone, encrypted.

Features

  • Remaining budget and spending by category.
  • Receipt in seconds: the fiscal QR is read offline, photos and PDFs are recognised on the device.
  • Review the receipt's line items: remove what does not belong, fix a price or quantity.
  • Apartment acceptance checklist.
  • CSV export.
