Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026. Applies to the Renovo app for iOS.

In short: Renovo does not collect, sell or share your data. Everything you enter or photograph is stored only on your device, encrypted. There are no analytics, ads, tracking or accounts. The only time the app goes online is to look up an official exchange rate, and only if you allow it.

Data the app processes

Project name, rooms and categories, expense amounts, vendor names, notes, receipt photos or PDFs, receipt line items, acceptance checklist results, and settings (language, Face ID lock, the currency totals are shown in, and whether exchange rates may be fetched online). You create all of it yourself and it is used only to make the app work.

Where data is stored

Only on your iPhone. The database and files are encrypted with keys kept in the device Keychain that never sync to iCloud. Files are excluded from backups. The developer has no access to your data and cannot technically obtain it.

What never leaves the device

  • Receipt photos and PDFs are recognised on the device with iOS frameworks (Vision, PDFKit). They are not uploaded anywhere.
  • The fiscal receipt QR code is parsed offline; the app does not contact the tax authority's website automatically.
  • The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.

Exchange rates

When you record an expense in another currency, or choose to see totals in another currency, Renovo can fill in the official exchange rate. Before the first request it asks for your permission; you can choose to enter rates yourself instead, and change this any time in Settings → Currencies.

If you allow it, the app sends a request to the National Bank of Ukraine (bank.gov.ua) or, for dollars and euros in US projects, to the European Central Bank reference-rate service Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev). The request contains only a date and a currency code (for example, "USD for 19 October 2026"). It contains no amounts, vendors, notes, names, account or device identifiers. As with any internet request, the server sees your IP address and the time of the request; Renovo does not control how these public services log requests. The rate received is stored on your device, encrypted, so the same day is not requested again.

Permissions

Camera: to read a QR code or photograph a receipt. Photos: to pick a receipt photo from the library. Face ID / Touch ID: optional, to lock the app. Permissions can be revoked in iOS Settings.

Export

CSV export creates a file that you share yourself through the system share sheet. That file is not encrypted; the app warns you before exporting.

Diagnostics mode

Settings contain a hidden diagnostics mode that helps the developer fix receipt-recognition mistakes. It is off by default. Turning it on requires Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode, after a warning that the journal will contain the text of your receipts.

While it is on, the app records in a journal on the device the text iOS recognised on your receipts (with the position of each line), the intermediate parsing results (total, date, vendor, line items, QR codes) and how long each step took, plus the app and iOS versions, the iPhone model and the database version. Receipt photos are never included. The journal is encrypted with its own Keychain key, takes at most 5 MB and is kept out of backups and system logs. The mode turns itself off 24 hours after it was turned on and the journal is erased; you can erase it sooner ("Disable and wipe journal"), and "Delete everything" removes it too.

The journal is never sent anywhere automatically. You can export it only yourself, through the system share sheet; the app first shows exactly which files will be shared and warns that they are not encrypted. Where they go is your choice.

Backups

You can make a backup of your projects and receipts in Settings → Backup. The backup is one encrypted file; Renovo hands it to the iOS share sheet and keeps no copy. You decide where it goes: Files on your iPhone, your iCloud Drive, AirDrop or anywhere else. Renovo has no server and never sees the file.

Everything inside the file is encrypted with AES-256. Its name contains only the date and time it was made. By default the key that opens it is kept in your iCloud Keychain, so your other iPhones signed in with the same Apple ID can restore it without typing anything; Apple stores that key end-to-end encrypted. If iCloud Keychain is off, such a backup opens only on the iPhone that made it; Renovo says so and offers to add a passphrase as a second key. You can instead protect backups with a passphrase only: then anyone who has the file and the passphrase can open it, and nobody else. Renovo does not store the passphrase and cannot recover it.

Restoring a backup replaces all data on the device only after you confirm it, and only after the whole file was checked for damage or changes. "Delete everything" does not touch your backup files or the backup key in your iCloud Keychain, so you can still restore a backup, for example after wiping an old phone. If you also tick "Also make my backups unreadable on all devices", the backup key is deleted from your iCloud Keychain on all your devices and backups protected with your Apple ID can never be opened again. Backups protected with a passphrase can still be opened with it; delete those files yourself if you no longer want them.

Deleting data

Settings → Delete everything destroys the encryption keys, the database and the files. Data cannot be recovered afterwards. Deleting the app from the device also deletes all data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from users of any age.

Changes

If the app ever processes data differently, for example with sync, this page will be updated before that version ships and the app will say so explicitly.
